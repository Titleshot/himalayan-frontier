      How we guide

      Every itinerary follows one rule: climb high, sleep low, and never rush a rest day.

      2×daily checks

      Oxygen checks above 3,500 m

      Your guide records pulse-oximeter readings every morning and evening from Namche, Manang or Samagaon upward, and changes the day if the numbers say so.

      1:2porter ratio

      Licensed guides, fairly paid porters

      Every guide holds a Nepal government trekking licence and first-aid training. One porter carries for every two trekkers, insured and properly equipped.

      0skipped rest days

      Acclimatisation days stay in the plan

      Rest days include a short climb and a return to sleep lower. They stay in the itinerary even when the group feels strong.

      24hevacuation cover

      A plan for the bad day

      Every group carries a satellite messenger. We coordinate helicopter evacuation with your insurer if a guide calls it.

        How we manage altitude

        • Above 3,000 m we raise the sleeping altitude by about 300–500 m a night.
        • A rest day for every 1,000 m gained, with a short climb high and a return to sleep low.
        • Pulse-oximeter checks morning and evening; guides carry a first-aid kit and a satellite messenger.
        • Headache, nausea or poor sleep: tell your guide. If symptoms get worse, the treatment is to go down.
        • Drink 3–4 litres a day and skip alcohol until you are descending.
        0 of 0 packed

        Down jacket and sleeping bag can be rented in Kathmandu or Pokhara. Porters carry up to 10–12 kg per trekker in a duffel we provide.

        Tourist visa on arrival

        • 15 days: US$30 · 30 days: US$50 · 90 days: US$125 (multiple entry).
        • Available at Kathmandu airport and main land borders. You can fill in the online form up to 15 days before you arrive.
        • Your passport must be valid for at least six months from arrival.
        • SAARC citizens (except Afghanistan) get a free 30-day visa.

        When you land

        • We meet you at Tribhuvan International Airport with a name board and take you to your hotel in Thamel.
        • Trek briefing and gear check the same evening or next morning.
        • For Everest treks, Lukla flights can leave from Ramechhap (Manthali) in peak season, a 4–5 hour drive from Kathmandu.
        • Keep one or two spare days before your flight home in case mountain flights are delayed.

        Visa fees as of 2026. Check the Department of Immigration before you travel.

        Your insurance must cover

        • Trekking or climbing up to the highest point of your trip (for example 5,545 m on Everest Base Camp).
        • Helicopter rescue and medical evacuation.
        • Trip cancellation and delays, including mountain flight delays.

        We ask for your policy number and the insurer’s 24-hour phone number before departure.

        Health

        • See your doctor or a travel clinic six to eight weeks before you go about vaccinations and altitude medication.
        • Drink only boiled, filtered or treated water. Teahouses sell boiled water for refilling bottles.
        • Train with long hill walks carrying a day pack for two to three months beforehand.
